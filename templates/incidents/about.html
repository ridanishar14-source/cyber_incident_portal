About CIRS

The Cyber Incident Reporting System (CIRS) is a dedicated platform built for the modern digital landscape, providing a secure bridge between affected users and security response teams.

Our Mission

To create a safer digital environment by providing a seamless, secure, and rapid channel for reporting cybersecurity threats. We aim to reduce the impact of digital attacks through early detection and coordinated intelligence.

Core Objectives

  • Ensure end-to-end encryption for all incident data.
  • Minimize response time through automated triage.
  • Provide actionable security intelligence to users.
  • Maintain transparency in the resolution process.

How It Works

01

Report Submission

Users submit detailed information about the incident, including type, severity, and any available digital evidence like screenshots or logs.

02

Expert Analysis

Our platform automatically prioritizes reports based on severity. Security analysts then review the data to determine the best course of action.

03

Resolution & Alert

Once investigated, users receive a status update. Critical patterns are used to generate global alerts to protect other users in real-time.
